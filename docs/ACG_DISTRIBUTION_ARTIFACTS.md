# ACG distribution artifacts

Use only artifacts generated by the current scripts below for English Etsy delivery.

- `python scripts/build_acg_distribution_artifacts.py`
  - `output/acg/current-en/nanamiastro-ACG-Premium-Bundle-Automatic-Access-EN-CURRENT.zip`
  - `output/acg/current-en/Chief-Editor-Neko-Personal-Edition-ACG-Sample-EN-CURRENT.zip`
- `python scripts/build_etsy_acg_access_pdf.py`
  - `output/pdf/nanami_acg_premium_bundle_etsy_en.pdf`
- `python scripts/build_personal_edition_acg_sample_pdf.py`
  - `output/pdf/personal-edition-acg-access-code-sample-en.pdf`
- `python media/etsy-acg-demo/build_video.py --ffmpeg <path>`
  - `media/etsy-acg-demo/etsy_acg_demo_15s.mp4`
  - English-normalized source frames in `media/etsy-acg-demo/frames-en/`
- `python scripts/build_etsy_acg_neko_video.py`
  - `output/video/etsy-acg/neko-chart-companion-demo-15s.mp4`
  - English-normalized source frames in `output/video/etsy-acg/source-en/`

The automatic-access ZIP is the standard Etsy buyer package. The access-code PDF is an exception-support sample, not the standard Etsy delivery flow.

## Legacy and QA outputs

Existing files whose names contain `Final`, `fixed`, or lack `CURRENT` under `output/` may represent older full-bundle, Japanese, or QA builds. They are preserved for compatibility and investigation, but must not be uploaded to an English listing. In particular, do not distribute:

- `output/ACG-All-Downloads-Lean-Final.zip`
- `output/acg/BirthChartMuseum-PersonalEdition-ACG-Bundle-fixed.zip`
- `output/Chief-Editor-Neko-Personal-Edition-ACG-Sample-EN.zip`

Regenerate from the scripts instead of renaming a legacy archive.
